The manifests with exact record counts and hashes are in each model repository (`pool-v3-manifest.json`, `pool-v5-manifest.json`); the training records themselves are not republished.

## Read this before any commercial use
- **Non-commercial sources are in the training data:** `lmsys/toxic-chat` (cc-by-nc-4.0), `facebook/anli` (cc-by-nc-4.0) and `allenai/sciq` (cc-by-nc-3.0). Whether a LoRA trained on them inherits that restriction is not settled; treat the adapters as research releases and take your own legal advice.
- **Share-alike sources** (CC BY-SA 3.0: DBpedia-14, FEVER, MultiNLI fiction) are also included.
- Several cards state no licence, or `unknown` or `other`; we recorded them as found.
- The base models are Apache-2.0 (Qwen3). The documents in this repository are CC BY 4.0; the adapters are not covered by that licence.

## Synthetic tickets (important)
780 support-ticket records in pool v3 are **synthetic and LLM-written**: they were generated by an LLM (Claude Haiku) from deterministic specifications for this project, and are not real customer data. Another 724 records come from the public `LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions` dataset, whose states are LLM-rendered with teacher-labelled gold labels (the card does not name the model). The ticket texts are LLM-written to specifications that fix the intended labels. Several public sources carry annotations produced by their authors' LLMs (for example UltraFeedback). We did not use Claude, GPT or another LLM to annotate records of pool v5 or to label data for these adapters.

## What was excluded, and why
- **Evaluation sources, kept out of training by a reserved list:** MMLU, MMLU-Pro, MedMCQA, CommonsenseQA, ARC, QASC, ScienceQA, QuaRTz, MedQA-USMLE, a CVE threat-intelligence hub, MINDS-14, BoolQ, SNLI, SciTail, HellaSwag. They are used (or reserved) as held-out evaluation sets, so training on them would invalidate the results.
- **Social IQa** was dropped from the pool because a BIG-bench family built on it is an evaluation set.
- **Contamination filtering** removed 546 of 48,466 candidate records that matched held-out evaluation inputs.
- **Bekko System One and Saihai datasets** were not used.
- **S1MB benchmark data** was not used for training, prompts, option orders, limits or checkpoint selection. See the S1MB submission page for the overlap that exists anyway through shared public sources.
