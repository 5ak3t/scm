# Training data and licences

The v1 adapters were trained on **pool v6 lite**: 50,464 records (56,280 questions: 40,596 choice, 11,870 yes/no, 3,814 score; 10.3% of the questions have 21 to 160 options). It has three parts. Licences below are those recorded at build time from each dataset card or upstream source (**recorded, not used as a filter**).

1. **A seeded sample of an earlier pool**: 26,991 records drawn from 53 sources (public datasets plus one small in-house set, the support tickets). Table 1.
2. **Training splits of 65 families from the Bekko System One dataset** (`hotchpotch/bekko-system-one-dataset-v0`, revision `9c673a09c31e145684ab9915871afa72ba40a383`): 21,675 records (24,362 decisions), at most 350 decisions per family and 1,000 for the wide intent families. Each family is converted from an upstream dataset. Table 2.
3. **Three extra wide-label sets**: 1,798 records with 50 to 100 labels, one per source. Table 3.

## Read this before any commercial use
- **Non-commercial sources are in the training data (sampled part):** `lmsys/toxic-chat` (cc-by-nc-4.0), `facebook/anli` (cc-by-nc-4.0) and `allenai/sciq` (cc-by-nc-3.0). Whether a LoRA trained on them inherits that restriction is not settled; treat the adapters as research-only until you have reviewed the licences.
- **Share-alike sources** (CC BY-SA) are included: DBpedia-14, FEVER and MultiNLI fiction in the sampled part, and the Bekko families arc, dbpedia, defeasible_nli, few_nerd, mtop, paws, sgd, snli and ud_ewt (as recorded in Table 2).
- Some records are unusual or unclear: one family is recorded as `gpl-3.0` (ethos), and several are recorded as `unknown`, "not verified", "not explicit" or with conflicting statements (argument_quality, creak, laya__ag_news, laya__enron_spam, scicite, open_jev__customer-control). We recorded them as found.
- The base models are Apache-2.0 (Qwen3). The documents in this repository are CC BY 4.0; the adapters are not covered by that licence.

## Model-written and LLM-generated data
- The sampled part includes 167 **support-ticket records written by an LLM** (Claude Haiku) from deterministic specifications for this project; they are not real customer data.
- It also includes 165 records from the training split of `LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions` and 108 from WANLI. Per their dataset descriptions, the typed-decisions text was written by a model and its labels come from a teacher endpoint, and WANLI examples were generated by a language model and then revised and labelled by crowdworkers.
- Some public sources have labels produced by models by their authors (for example UltraFeedback).
- We did not generate any training label with an LLM for this training run. LLM-generated Bekko families were left out (see below).

## What was excluded, and why
- **LLM-generated Bekko material:** the `gretel_pii` family (generated with a language model), the `laya__typed_decisions` family (model-written text, teacher labels), decisions labelled by an LLM jury, and refusal-augmentation decisions were not used. Bekko configs without an S1MB test split (including GPT-labelled retrieval sets and other synthetic sets) were not used. Only English and undetermined-language rows were kept.
- **Evaluation sources of our own open evaluation sets** (for example MMLU, MMLU-Pro, MedMCQA, CommonsenseQA, ScienceQA, QASC, BoolQ, HellaSwag, and a CVE threat-intelligence hub) stay out of the sampled part by a reserved list. Social IQa was dropped because a BIG-bench family built on it is an evaluation set. v1 **deliberately adds the training splits of datasets that S1MB is built from**, including ARC and SNLI, through the Bekko part.
- **Decontamination against S1MB test inputs (inputs only, no labels):** of 28,001 candidate new records, 4,524 were dropped (113 exact matches, 2,885 by 8-word shingle overlap with Jaccard at least 0.5, and 1,526 where at least half of a test input's shingles sit inside the training item), and 4 more for matching our own evaluation inputs. 9 of the 27,000 sampled records were dropped as test-input duplicates.
- **S1MB data:** only training splits of the source datasets were used. S1MB test inputs were read only to remove matching training items; no S1MB test label or score was used for training, prompts, option orders, limits or checkpoint selection. The overlap that remains through shared public sources is described on the S1MB submission page.
